Our Story

A 40-year food-trade legacy — now manufacturing.

Goyal Ji is the manufacturing arm of JP Industries, an enterprise rooted in four decades of India's food trade — from red chilli trading and cold storage to spice processing, and today processed-food manufacturing. We don't make food because it's a new market for us; we make it because we've spent a generation inside the supply chain that surrounds it.
