The Heritage

Goyal Ji is the manufacturing arm of JP Industries, an enterprise rooted in four decades of India's food trade. The journey moved from red chilli trading, to cold storage & warehousing, to spice processing, and finally into processed food manufacturing — each step adding infrastructure, process discipline, and category knowledge. We don't make food because it's a new market for us; we make it because we've spent a generation inside the supply chain that surrounds it.
